Privacy

Private period tracking without an account

Period data can feel private even when it looks ordinary on a calendar. MiniCycle therefore avoids account-based tracking for the core app experience.

Collect less by design

MiniCycle stores period dates and settings on the device. Local storage reduces the infrastructure that can touch cycle history and keeps the product focused on a calendar, not an account system.

What local storage does not solve

Local storage is not a medical privacy guarantee. Device backups, screenshots, shared devices, and operating system settings still matter. The product choice is narrower: avoid unnecessary server accounts and remote cycle storage.

Website analytics are separate

This website uses Google Analytics measurement ID G-339Q79533S for page analytics. Blog page views do not include in-app period dates, notes, or cycle history.
