MiniCycle iCloud Sync not working? Check these before deleting your records

Published September 5, 2026·by Chanwook Kim, MiniCycle developer·3 min read

At a glance

If iCloud Sync seems stuck, start with the message below the switch in MiniCycle Settings. Check your Apple Account, connection, and available iCloud storage, then reopen MiniCycle and look for a recent sync time. Keep the device that still has your records until you have checked that the information you need is present.

Start with the status below the switch

Open MiniCycle Settings and find iCloud Sync in the Data section. It is optional and off by default. Simply signing in to an Apple Account does not turn on this setting inside MiniCycle.

While a sync is running, the app shows a progress message. Before the first successful sync, it may show a preparation message. After a successful upload or restore, it shows the last sync time. An error appears in the same place. A switch that is on tells you sync is enabled; it does not by itself confirm that your latest changes have been saved.

Check the account and connection

If the message asks you to check your iCloud sign-in, open the iPhone Settings app and check the Apple Account shown at the top. When restoring records on another iPhone, it needs to use the same account that stored them.

Apple Support explains where to review app access under Settings, your name, then iCloud and the list of saved apps. If MiniCycle appears there, check whether it is allowed to use iCloud. For a network error, connect to a working Wi-Fi or cellular network, then return to MiniCycle. You can still make local records without an internet connection; saving those changes to iCloud requires one.

Check iCloud storage, not just iPhone storage

If MiniCycle reports that iCloud storage is full, check the usage shown in Settings under your name and iCloud. Space available on the iPhone and space available in iCloud are separate. Deleting something only from the phone may not solve an iCloud storage problem.

Apple’s storage guide explains how to review what is using the space. Keep a copy of anything you need before removing cloud content. Do not delete MiniCycle records or its cloud data merely to test whether synchronization will start working.

Reopen the app and check what actually arrived

After addressing the account, connection, or storage issue, reopen MiniCycle with iCloud Sync enabled. The app checks for cloud data when opened and attempts to save local changes while sync is enabled. Let the current operation finish, then check the message and last sync time again. Avoid repeatedly flipping the switch while troubleshooting.

A recent sync time describes a successful operation on that device. It does not prove that another iPhone has already received the same records. On a device you are restoring to, also check a recent period start, its end date, and a note you recognize before considering the transfer complete.

Keep your existing copy while troubleshooting

A delayed sync is not a reason to reset all data or delete and reinstall MiniCycle. Records are stored locally by default, so removing the app before confirming another copy can put those records at risk. Keep the iPhone that still shows them available.

MiniCycle’s iCloud feature stores a saved state of records and settings. It does not provide a screen for choosing an older backup version, and you should not assume that independently edited records from two phones will be combined. While checking a problem, avoid making different corrections on both devices.

If an error keeps returning

Messages about unreadable cloud data, a setup problem, or a failed save may need further investigation. Write down the exact message and approximately when it appeared. Note your MiniCycle version, iOS version, and whether the issue followed a phone change or an interrupted connection.

Use the MiniCycle support page to contact the developer with those details. Start with the error message and steps that reproduce it. There is no need to send period dates, symptom notes, Apple Account credentials, or other private records in an initial support request.
