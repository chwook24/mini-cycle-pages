Cycle length and period length statistics

Cycle statistics are useful when they stay humble. MiniCycle shows simple recent-record numbers instead of turning every variation into a score.

What cycle length means

Cycle length is counted from the first day of one period to the first day of the next. ACOG patient guidance describes common ranges, but individual patterns vary. MiniCycle uses saved start dates to estimate your recent rhythm.

What period length means

Period length is the number of bleeding days saved for a period. MiniCycle estimates this from closed ranges and lets manual settings take priority when you know your own typical length.

Why median-style values help

A plain average can move too much after one unusual cycle. A median-style calculation is less sensitive to one very short or long interval, which is why MiniCycle trims outliers when enough data exists.
