What a period calendar widget should show on the iPhone home screen

A widget is not a compressed app. It should answer one question quickly: where am I in my cycle today?

Glanceable information

Apple describes widgets as focused surfaces for timely information. MiniCycle follows that idea by showing current cycle status, a compact calendar, predicted period days, and fertile-window highlights without turning the widget into a form.

Why less is better

Dense health widgets can become hard to read and may expose private details on the home screen. MiniCycle keeps notes and editing inside the app, while the widget remains a quick check surface.

Shared calculation

The app calendar and widget use the same prediction logic. This matters because people quickly lose trust when the widget and app disagree.
