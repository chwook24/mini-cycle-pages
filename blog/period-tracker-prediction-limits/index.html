Prediction limits

When period tracker predictions are useful, and when they are not

A calendar estimate can be useful without being certain. MiniCycle shows helpful dates while making limits visible.

Useful for planning

Predictions can help you prepare for an expected period, notice when a cycle is drifting, and remember past start dates. That planning value is the main reason to use a simple period calendar.

Not contraception

Calendar estimates should not be treated as contraception. Fertility awareness methods require specific rules, consistent observation, and often barrier protection or abstinence during fertile days when avoiding pregnancy.

Not a diagnosis

Late, early, painful, heavy, or unusual bleeding can have many explanations. A tracker can help you bring dates to a clinician, but it cannot diagnose the reason behind a change.
