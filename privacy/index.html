MiniCycle

Privacy Policy

MiniCycle is designed to collect as little as possible for a simple period calendar.

App records

Period dates, notes, sexual activity markers, and cycle settings are stored locally on your device by default. MiniCycle does not require a MiniCycle account.

Optional iCloud Sync

If you turn on iCloud Sync in the app settings, MiniCycle stores a copy of your app records and settings in your private iCloud account using Apple CloudKit so you can restore them on a new iPhone signed in to the same Apple ID. MiniCycle does not operate its own sync server.

App analytics

MiniCycle includes Firebase Analytics to understand app usage patterns such as app opens, screen and tab navigation, calendar interaction, privacy-safe record action categories, setting changes, notification permission results, and optional iCloud sync status. These events are designed not to include period dates, memo text, sexual activity markers, exact cycle settings, exact notification times, or cycle history.

Website analytics

The MiniCycle website uses Google Analytics measurement ID G-339Q79533S to understand page views, traffic sources, and blog usage. Website analytics do not include in-app period dates, notes, or cycle history.
