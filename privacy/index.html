MiniCycle

Privacy Policy

Effective date: May 6, 2026

Overview

MiniCycle is designed to store your cycle records on your device. The app does not require an account and does not send your cycle records to the developer.

Data We Do Not Collect

MiniCycle does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal data with the developer or third parties. The app does not include advertising SDKs, analytics SDKs, account login, or server sync.

Data Stored On Your Device

The app may store the following information locally on your device:

  • Period start and end dates
  • Daily memo text
  • Sexual activity marker
  • Cycle, period, calendar, appearance, privacy, language, and notification settings

This data is stored using iOS local storage and an App Group container so the MiniCycle widget can show your current cycle information. The data is not sent to the developer.

Notifications

If you enable notifications, MiniCycle schedules local notifications on your device for predicted period, fertile window, fertile window end, or ovulation dates. Notification settings and scheduled notifications remain on your device.

Face ID

MiniCycle can use Face ID to protect the setting that reveals sexual activity records. Face ID authentication is handled by iOS. MiniCycle does not receive or store biometric data.

Health Disclaimer

MiniCycle provides estimates based on the records and settings you enter. Predictions are for informational purposes only and are not medical advice. They should not be used as a substitute for professional medical guidance, contraception, fertility treatment, diagnosis, or emergency care.
